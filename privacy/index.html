Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what Facts Should Know ("we", "us") does and does not do with personal information. It covers this website and the software we use to publish our own content to social media.

1. This website

  • There are no user accounts, no comments and no advertising on this site, and we do not set cookies of our own.
  • Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep ordinary server logs (such as your IP address, the page you asked for and your browser type) for security and operations. We do not control those logs.
  • We use an analytics script to count visits: which pages are read, the referring site, the country and the browser type. We use it to see what people read, not to identify individual visitors.
  • Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you follow one and buy something, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Once you leave this site, the merchant's own privacy policy and cookies apply.

2. The software that publishes our content

We use software that writes our posts (text, images and short videos) and publishes them to accounts that we own on social platforms such as YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, Bluesky, Mastodon and Telegram. It is used only by us to run our own accounts. It is not a service offered to the public, and it has no sign-up for visitors.

  • How it connects. Only through each platform's official API, using access that the account owner granted on that platform's own sign-in and consent screen.
  • What it can access. Only our own accounts. It does not access the accounts, posts, messages, contacts or personal data of any other person, visitor, follower or subscriber. It reads the aggregate numbers a platform reports for our own posts (such as views, likes, comments and shares counts).
  • What it stores. The access credentials for our own accounts, kept encrypted on our own server, and a record of the posts we made and their aggregate numbers. Nothing about other people is collected, so there is nothing about them to store.
  • What it does not do. We do not sell data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising.
  • Google APIs. Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access. The owner of an account can remove our access at any time from the security or connected-apps settings of that platform. Doing so stops the software from posting there.

3. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Your choices

If you have sent us personal information, for example by email, you can ask us to tell you what we hold or to delete it. Write to facts.should.know.26@gmail.com.

If we change this policy we will change the date at the top of this page.
